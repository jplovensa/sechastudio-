Our affiliation / The material journal

A shared direction.
A considered interior.

Good spaces begin with people. They take shape through the materials we choose.

SECHA is affiliated with Fjäll Group through Greenshift. This connection sits alongside our studio’s belief that an interior should feel personal, work beautifully and be thoughtfully put together.

For our modular approach, EPS-based materials form the lightweight core of selected interior panels. The surface is only one part of the story: finishes, supports and fixing details are considered together, around the needs of your space.

From the first brief to the final detail, we bring the conversation back to how you live. A material direction becomes meaningful when it becomes a room that belongs to you.

How We Work

Step 01

Getting to Know You

We start with a conversation. We listen to your needs, understand how you live, and align our vision with your budget and space realities.

Step 02

The Vision

Your ideas turn into a visual plan. We create bespoke moodboards and clear 2D layouts so you can see exactly how the space will be utilised.

Step 03

Bringing It to Life

No more guessing. We provide high-quality 3D renders that show you exactly what your finished room will look like before execution.

Step 04

The Blueprint

We handle the technical details. You receive detailed engineering drawings (DED) so contractors know exactly how to build the design flawlessly.

Step 05

Making it Happen

The final handover. Take our plans to your builder, or transition seamlessly into our bespoke furniture production.
