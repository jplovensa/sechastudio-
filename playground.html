A little guide to your playground

Build: choose a piece and tap the floor. Drag placed pieces to move them. Use the selected-piece controls to rotate or remove.

Explore: switch to Walk through, tap an open spot, or use WASD / arrow keys. Walk near furniture and press E to sit, care for plants, make coffee, switch lamps or play table games. Stand up before walking again. Your resident stays inside the room and avoids furniture.

Look around: drag empty floor to orbit in Build mode. Scroll or use + / − to zoom. Use Plan for a view from above.

Use the placed-pieces list and arrow buttons to edit with a keyboard. Save stores this project in this browser; Export downloads a layout file.
